# 🧠 Health Awareness & Neurodiversity Infographics

Welcome to this repository! This project aims to simplify complex medical, neurodevelopmental, and maternal-fetal health concepts using visual infographics and research-backed data.

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains educational research posters designed to spread awareness about:
1. **Down Syndrome & Brain Development** in children.
2. **Advanced Maternal Age (35+) & Associated Health Risks** for infants.

---

## 1. 🧬 Down Syndrome & The Brain (Child Development)

This infographic highlights how the brain of a child with Down Syndrome differs structurally and functionally from a typical brain, emphasizing their unique learning strengths.

### 🖼️ Poster Preview

![Down Syndrome Brain Poster](down_syndrome_brain.png)

### 🔑 Key Structural & Cognitive Takeaways
* **Cerebellum (Coordination & Balance):** Typically smaller in size, affecting motor skills, posture, and speech articulation.
* **Hippocampus (Memory Center):** Lower cell density, leading to challenges with short-term memory and sequential learning.
* **Frontal Lobe (Planning & Attention):** Lower synaptic density impacting decision-making, executive functions, and expressive language.
* **Visual Learning Strength:** Children with Down Syndrome process **visual information** much better than auditory instructions.

## 2. 📊 Advanced Maternal Age (35+) & Infant Health Risks

This research-based infographic details the neurodevelopmental, chromosomal, and physical risks associated with pregnancies at or above age 35.

### 🖼️ Poster Preview

![Advanced Maternal Age Poster](maternal_age_risks.png)

### 🔬 Summary of Key Research Findings
1. **Chromosomal Errors (Trisomy 21 / Down Syndrome):** Increased rates of meiotic non-disjunction during oocyte maturation.
2. **Neurodevelopmental Risks:** Statistically higher incidence of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and executive function delays.
3. **Prematurity & Birth Weight:** Higher incidence of maternal preeclampsia/gestational diabetes leading to premature birth (<37 weeks) and low birth weight.
4. **Congenital Heart Defects (CHD):** Elevated risk requiring early fetal echocardiography and targeted anomaly scans.
